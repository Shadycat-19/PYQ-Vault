Image Upload Guidelines

Follow these best practices to ensure your uploaded question papers and solutions are easy for other students to read and study.

Clear & Sharp Focus
Ensure all questions, equations, diagrams, and marks are sharply in focus. Avoid blurry, shaky, or low-resolution shots.
Flat Top-Down Angle & Lighting
Hold your device directly above the paper (parallel to the desk). Ensure bright, even lighting without fingers or hand shadows.
Sequential Page Order
Upload pages in correct chronological order (Page 1, 2, ...). You can reorder pages using the arrow buttons before uploading.
Supported Formats & Documents
Supports PDF, Word (.docx), text (.txt), and PNG/JPG images. Multi-page PDFs and Word files are automatically rendered into high-res pages.
